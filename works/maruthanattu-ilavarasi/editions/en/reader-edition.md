<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u001; source:PDF 2 / printed 1 -->
**Written text**  
Dialogue: M. Karunanidhi.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u002; source:PDF 2 / printed 1 -->
**Narrative**  
The ruler of Kurinji country had two wives. Both were pregnant. Durjayan, the elder brother of the younger queen, had one particular desire: to preserve his power, his sister's child should rule the land. Because of that.................

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u003; source:PDF 2 / printed 1 -->
**கோபதிவர்மன்**  
Murderess. Enough of your antics. After carrying out sabotage there, you dance here to cover it up.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u004; source:PDF 2 / printed 1 -->
**சித்ரா**  
What?

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u005; source:PDF 2 / printed 1 -->
**கோபதி**  
You made a plan to ruin Singari's life—do you know that plan is dead? You wretch.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u006; source:PDF 2 / printed 1 -->
**சித்ரா**  
A plan? What plan?

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u007; source:PDF 2 / printed 1 -->
**கோபதி**  
Ah! What acting, you wicked woman! Even throwing you into the fire and burning you to ashes would not be enough.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u008; source:PDF 2 / printed 1 -->
**அன்பானந்தர்**  
Your Majesty, anger will obscure justice. Calmly....

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u009; source:PDF 2 / printed 1 -->
**கோபதி**  
Calmly, Anbanandar! You yourself question this Neeli!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u010; source:PDF 2 / printed 1 -->
**அன்பா**  
Maharani, you are jealous because the younger queen is pregnant; you fear that child may claim a share in the kingdom. Because of that, you mixed poison into the medicine sent to the younger queen.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u011; source:PDF 3 / printed 2 -->
**சித்ரா**  
Ah!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u012; source:PDF 3 / printed 2 -->
**அன்பா**  
This is the younger Maharani's accusation.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u013; source:PDF 3 / printed 2 -->
**சித்ரா**  
Do you believe this?

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u014; source:PDF 3 / printed 2 -->
**கோபதி**  
I believe it completely. Chitra, not just Singari—you would dare even to kill me for your own selfish ends!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u015; source:PDF 3 / printed 2 -->
**சித்ரா**  
An outrageous fabrication. I am innocent! Do not tear my heart apart.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u016; source:PDF 3 / printed 2 -->
**கோபதி**  
Tch! Do not touch me. (He pushes her down.)

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u017; source:PDF 3 / printed 2 -->
**அன்பா**  
Your Majesty! She is fully pregnant.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u018; source:PDF 3 / printed 2 -->
**கோபதி**  
Pregnant! Singari too is pregnant.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u019; source:PDF 3 / printed 2 -->
**சித்ரா**  
My lord, I regard Singari as my younger sister.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u020; source:PDF 3 / printed 2 -->
**கோபதி**  
A lie—an utter lie!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u021; source:PDF 3 / printed 2 -->
**சித்ரா**  
There is no one to plead for me. Singari has her brother on her side. And she has the king, who is dear to her. Truth alone is my witness!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u022; source:PDF 3 / printed 2 -->
**அன்பா**  
Maharaja! To clarify the matter, both the complainant and the accused must be questioned.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u023; source:PDF 3 / printed 2 -->
**கோபதி**  
A vain hope. Even so, let no one say the ruler of Kurinji country acted in haste. You yourself arrange the court.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u024; source:PDF 3 / printed 2 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
Kalinga, the matter has gone wrong. What we expected did not happen. Tomorrow in court our plots will be exposed. A grave will be built for this Durjayan's absolute power. My desire that my sister's son should rule this land will be destroyed. If we are to recover from this, the Maharani must die.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u025; source:PDF 4 / printed 3 -->
**காளிங்கன்**  
Yes!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u026; source:PDF 4 / printed 3 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
Our plan must be carried out tonight itself.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u027; source:PDF 4 / printed 3 -->
* * *

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u028; source:PDF 4 / printed 3 -->
**அன்பா**  
Our plan must be carried out tonight itself.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u029; source:PDF 4 / printed 3 -->
**சித்ரா**  
Then must I flee the palace?

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u030; source:PDF 4 / printed 3 -->
**அன்பா**  
Yes, my lady! To escape this danger, you have no choice but to leave.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u031; source:PDF 4 / printed 3 -->
**சித்ரா**  
Rather than leave and deepen the king's sorrow, it would be better for me to die.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u032; source:PDF 4 / printed 3 -->
**அன்பா**  
No, Maharani! Your own life is not what matters most. The jewel growing in your womb is fit for the crown of Kurinji country. To save it, you must make this sacrifice;

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u033; source:PDF 4 / printed 3 -->
**சித்ரா**  
Where can I go, Minister!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u034; source:PDF 4 / printed 3 -->
**அன்பா**  
Go through the tunnel. It will take you to the nearby village.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u035; source:PDF 4 / printed 3 -->
**சித்ரா**  
Minister!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u036; source:PDF 4 / printed 3 -->
**அன்பா**  
Do not worry. As long as injustice lives in the palace, live incognito; afterward, justice itself will draw you back to the palace.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u037; source:PDF 4 / printed 3 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
Ah! She has escaped!!......

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u038; source:PDF 4 / printed 3 -->
**அன்பா**  
Check the pillow carefully. Kalinga, check it thoroughly............ Kalinga—ah, hahahaha!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-opening-u039; source:PDF 4 / printed 3 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
Laugh! Laugh well!.... Laugh out loud!... Laugh with a peal!... This is your last laugh, isn't it?... Kalinga, no one's eyes must see this fool's corpse. It must be buried in the palace garden.

## Scene 2

**English record:** `works/maruthanattu-ilavarasi/translations/records/scene-002.json`
**Tamil source derivative:** `works/maruthanattu-ilavarasi/scenes/scene-002.md`

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u001; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**சேவகன்**  
Maharaja! We searched everywhere, but the Maharani could not be found.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u002; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**கோபதி**  
Ah! She could not be found?

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u003; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
Not only that, Maharaja—the minister too cannot be found!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u004; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**கோபதி**  
The man who went searching has not returned?

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u005; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
He would have to have gone searching in order to return!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u006; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**கோபதி**  
Durjaya, what are you saying?

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u007; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
Is this something that even needs saying! Maharaja! When it comes to the Maharani, the minister has a special affection!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u008; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**கோபதி**  
Yes! That is why he set out to search without our even telling him.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u009; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
The minister has a special ambition in the government! Even more than that, an extraordinary love for the Maharani!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u010; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**கோபதி**  
I too showed love to that demoness.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u011; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
The king's love paled by comparison; the minister's love bewitched the Maharani!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u012; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**கோபதி**  
What?

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u013; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
His love is such that he can regard palace life and the office of minister as trifles!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u014; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**கோபதி**  
Ah! An outrage impossible even to imagine!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u015; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
Yet it has happened.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u016; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**கோபதி**  
Shame—great shame!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u017; source:PDF 5 / printed 4 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
If foreign lands hear of it, that will be an even greater shame!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s002-u018; source:PDF 6 / printed 5 -->
**கோபதி**  
Little foxes have plotted inside the lion's cave! Straws have played at the sword-point. Though they knew who Gopativarman was, those wicked, unjust wretches committed this outrage in his own palace. Durjaya, let the search for those traitors proceed relentlessly. Proclaim it throughout the land with drumbeat. Hack that adulteress into pieces and show her before my eyes. Wash away the stain that has fallen on Kurinji country with the blood of those wastrels. Whoever has hidden those great sinners—whoever they may be—death penalty! Torture! Hmm, let it be done.

## Scene 3

**English record:** `works/maruthanattu-ilavarasi/translations/records/scene-003.json`
**Tamil source derivative:** `works/maruthanattu-ilavarasi/scenes/scene-003.md`

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s003-u001; source:PDF 6 / printed 5 -->
**ராணி**  
Father!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s003-u002; source:PDF 6 / printed 5 -->
**மணிபல்லவர்**  
My child! Look at the wonderful gift you have found for your father. Your father must give up country and city alike and go to the forest.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s003-u003; source:PDF 6 / printed 5 -->
**ராணி**  
(She looks at the letter.) Maniballava, O king! A woman or war! Your consent is required.
Gopativarman.
Ah! What a valiant warning. Father! Write the reply—War! Write `War`.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s003-u004; source:PDF 6 / printed 5 -->
**மணி**  
Enough—the trouble brought by your own tongue! The chick's fault has fallen upon the hen's head.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s003-u005; source:PDF 6 / printed 5 -->
**ராணி**  
Why are you grieving, Father? Even an ant confronts its enemy!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s003-u006; source:PDF 6 / printed 5 -->
**மணி**  
Rani! I do not want to redden the waters of the Kaveri. I have no heart to turn the warriors of Marutha country into corpses. For the peace of the country, you must marry Raudhran.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s003-u007; source:PDF 7 / printed 6 -->
**ராணி**  
Father!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s003-u008; source:PDF 7 / printed 6 -->
**மணி**  
Can you not make even this sacrifice for me?

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s003-u009; source:PDF 7 / printed 6 -->
**ராணி**  
For you I would sacrifice my life. Honour is not something to be sacrificed, Father!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s003-u010; source:PDF 7 / printed 6 -->
**மணி**  
Rani! If victory is on their side, what becomes of our honour?

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s003-u011; source:PDF 7 / printed 6 -->
**ராணி**  
Victory! That is a decision the blood of warriors must determine! Why worry about it now?

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s003-u012; source:PDF 7 / printed 6 -->
**மணி**  
I know the strength of the warriors and my own strength. I do not need your preaching.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s003-u013; source:PDF 7 / printed 6 -->
**ராணி**  
Father! Are you the one speaking like this! Born in a lineage that says spear and sword are companions in life, are you speaking like this! Born in the heroic Pandyan line that declared, `In the pale cruelty of the field of death, the shoulders that sport are our victorious shoulders`, are you speaking like this? He who blinks at the thrust of a spear is a coward! Born of the Tamil who made this his heart-song, are you speaking like this? Shame—disgrace—disgrace to the history of our country itself. The sun that has seen many battlefields will laugh! This earth that bore warriors will laugh. No, Father! Let there be no stain on the history of valour. The land will laugh that Maniballavar, afraid of death, gave his daughter as tribute to the enemy. Let that stain not fall.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s003-u014; source:PDF 7→8 / printed 6→7 -->
**மணி**  
No, Rani! Do not hammer my unfeeling heart with a sledgehammer. You have turned me, who wriggled like a worm, into a tiger! I will not throw what I guard like my own eye into that bear's mouth! O crowing Kurinji country! No woman for you! War—to curb your swagger!

## Scene 4

**English record:** `works/maruthanattu-ilavarasi/translations/records/scene-004.json`
**Tamil source derivative:** `works/maruthanattu-ilavarasi/scenes/scene-004.md`

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s004-u001; source:PDF 8 / printed 7 -->
**கோபதி**  
War! Such swollen arrogance in that man of Marutha country. Though he knows we have armies like the waves of the sea, the fool has dared to enter the battlefield! Prince! Let the preparations for war begin today. Let the weapons and materiel of war be renewed.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s004-u002; source:PDF 8 / printed 7 -->
**ரௌத்ரன்**  
I ask it right now: I shall be the commander of this invasion.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s004-u003; source:PDF 8 / printed 7 -->
**கோபதி**  
Gladly! Destroy the border fort of Marutha country. Make the flag of Kurinji country fly over Maniballavan's palace.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s004-u004; source:PDF 8 / printed 7 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
When there is a way to wear the garland of victory without a knife and without shedding blood........

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s004-u005; source:PDF 8 / printed 7 -->
**ரௌத்**  
What way, Uncle?

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s004-u006; source:PDF 8 / printed 7 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
Invade while the enemies are asleep. Attack from hiding. This method of war costs little blood, but requires a little work from the brain.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s004-u007; source:PDF 8 / printed 7 -->
**ரௌத்**  
What Uncle says is a magnificent philosophy.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s004-u008; source:PDF 8 / printed 7 -->
**கோபதி**  
A very fine philosophy indeed. Let him act as he wishes.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s004-u009; source:PDF 8 / printed 7 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
I do not need marriage now. Rani! Now I will see about that arrogance.

O crowing Kurinji country—no woman for you. War! Maniballavar, the flag of Kurinji country is flying over your palace.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s005-u004; source:PDF 9 / printed 8 -->

That day you roared as though the sea-gate had burst open! Look—here is the handsome man you scorned! He has come to enslave you! As a warrior of warriors! As a lion of victory! (Laughter) As your life-partner!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s005-u005; source:PDF 9 / printed 8 -->
**ராணி**

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s005-u006; source:PDF 9 / printed 8 -->

I will make you agree! What do you say now!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s005-u009; source:PDF 9 / printed 8 -->

No—let him go.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s005-u010; source:PDF 9 / printed 8 -->

Impossible! Unless you agree to the marriage, I cannot let him go.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s005-u011; source:PDF 9 / printed 8 -->
**ராணி**  
I agree. Let him go!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s005-u012; source:PDF 9 / printed 8 -->
**துர்ஜயன்**  
We travel tomorrow—get ready! If you try to escape before then, your father's head will roll on the ground. Beware! Put this man under guard!

## Scene 6

**English record:** `works/maruthanattu-ilavarasi/translations/records/scene-006.json`
**Tamil source derivative:** `works/maruthanattu-ilavarasi/scenes/scene-006.md`

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s006-u001; source:PDF 9 / printed 8 -->
**காண்டிபன்**  
Where is Rani? Where? Rani! Princess Rani! Is my Rani a princess!.............

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s006-u002; source:PDF 10 / printed 9 -->
**ராணி**  
Yes! A princess indeed.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s006-u003; source:PDF 10 / printed 9 -->
**காண்**  
You said you were a servant, you masquerader! Wretch! That lust is surging in your eyes! That poison is dripping from the corners of your lips! With these, you have shattered my trust atom by atom, palace demon!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s006-u004; source:PDF 10 / printed 9 -->
**ராணி**  
To win your love.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s006-u005; source:PDF 10 / printed 9 -->
**காண்**  
Love! The name of the curtain you drew over your lust-mad desire is love! The stench of rot given the name of evening-jasmine fragrance—ah! I was deceived, taking Halahala poison for nectar! I mistook sunset for sunrise and was ruined to the root! If there is such a thing as hell, I would set a snake upon your veins and have it bite you... Even then there would be no settling this deceiver's betrayal of trust. Princess—merciless demon! A Brahma-rakshasi born in a royal line that torments the poor, eats their bones, and polishes its body with them! You bewitched me with your beauty, shattered my young heart with the charm of your words, and left me stranded in the street, destroyer! The fault is not yours! Kings hunger to seize ever-new kingdoms! A king's daughter hungers to seize ever-new husbands!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s006-u006; source:PDF 10 / printed 9 -->
**ராணி**  
I command you as princess: please leave!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s006-u007; source:PDF 10 / printed 9 -->
**காண்**  
I am leaving, but I do not intend to go empty-handed. I will leave only after plucking out those eyes that inflamed me with desire and drove me mad! Not content with leaving the man who trusted you stranded midstream, you ordered him to leave; I will leave only after tearing out that iron heart. (He strangles her.)

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s006-u008; source:PDF 11 / printed 10 -->
**ரம்பா**  
Soldiers! Soldiers!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s006-u009; source:PDF 11 / printed 10 -->
**ராணி**  
Run away this way.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s006-u010; source:PDF 11 / printed 10 -->
**ரௌத்**  
What conspiracy are you plotting? Where did he come from?

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s006-u011; source:PDF 11 / printed 10 -->
**ராணி**  
He did not come after asking me.

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s006-u012; source:PDF 11 / printed 10 -->
**ரௌத்**  
The feast meant for my sword has escaped. If that wastrel had fallen into my hands, I would have made him unrecognizable!

<!-- unit:maruthanattu-ilavarasi-en-s006-u013; source:PDF 11 / printed 10 -->

Let my eyes blaze once and then go dark! Let my blood heat and boil, then turn cold! O rich land of noble Tamil that lived as the ground of justice and the furnace of integrity!
