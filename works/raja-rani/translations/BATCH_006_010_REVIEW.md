## Review decisions

### Embedded `சேரன் செங்குட்டுவன்` ending

Scenes 6–7 retain the embedded historical-drama register and the return to the theatre audience. `Kuyilaluvam`, `Kannagi`, `Tamilakam`, `rishis`, `thambi`, `Vanakkam` and `pesh` remain where replacing them would flatten source register or comedy. Samarasam's rambling anti-war speech remains deliberately repetitive rather than being polished into a concise modern speech.

Scene 7 preserves the source's `டாட்டர்` / `டாக்டர்` daughter-doctor misunderstanding. The long source label `கீதாவின் தாய் தாயம்மாள்` remains immutable metadata through its dialogue-record link; the translation does not normalize it to the character index.

### Scene 8

The verified segment contains only the parenthetical statement that Babu attempts to rape Rani in the collection room. The English keeps that direct source meaning and does not soften, elaborate or invent action beyond the printed line.

### Scene 9 — written text and unlabelled speech

Scene 9 is the first Raja Rani English scene requiring source-unlabelled spoken units and substantial written matter.

The newspaper headline fragments remain `written-text`. The following reaction, `ஊம், லீலா...`, the quoted repetition from the letter, and the pillow-giving speech remain `dialogue`-kind units with both `speaker_label` and `source_record_id` null. Dramatic context is not promoted into invented source metadata.

The full newspaper letter to Leela remains one `written-text` unit across PDF **22→23**, with English page segments preserving the physical source break. It is not converted into Raja dialogue merely because he is reading the newspaper.

The `படி` read/step joke and the `ராஜா` name/title misunderstanding are preserved explicitly. Source-visible English code-switching (`steps`, `drama`, `act`, `house-full`, `excuse me`, `news`) remains visible rather than being silently naturalized.
