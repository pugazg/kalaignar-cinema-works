# ராஜா ராணி — Source-Supported Scene Segmentation Audit

## Purpose

This audit defines the archival scene segmentation for the screenplay range of `ராஜா ராணி` after completion of the rendered-scan fidelity gate.

The booklet does **not** print numbered screenplay scenes. Therefore:

- `T###` below are audit-row identifiers only;
- `raja-rani-s###` are archive-only navigation identifiers;
- neither is claimed as source numbering;
- page breaks alone do not create scene boundaries;
- boundaries are accepted only from source-visible separators/headings, explicit stage/location transitions, or clear dramatic transitions supported by the source.

The rendered scan remains controlling. The canonical page layer supplies the verified transcription used to interpret the structural evidence.

## Eligibility rule

The full screenplay has been visually audited, but PDF **27, 48, 57 and 74** remain review because the supplied scan cannot support complete verification there.

Under `tamil-fidelity-gate-disposition.md`:

- the structural scene index may cover the entire screenplay;
- a verified scene-text derivative may be produced only when the complete scene span is supported by verified Tamil;
- any scene intersecting PDF 27, 48, 57 or 74 is `blocked-source-review` for verified scene-text output.

## Separator ownership policy

For future scene-text files:

- a boundary separator belongs to structural provenance and is not expanded into prose;
- do not create synthetic `(Scene ends.)` text;
- do not duplicate the same separator at both the end of one derivative and the beginning of the next;
- source headings such as `★ நீதி மன்றம் ★`, `★ சிறை ★`, `★ ராணி வீடு ★` and `★ தோட்டம் ★` may be retained as source-visible heading material in the scene where they occur;
- archive reader labels are navigation aids and are not source headings unless explicitly marked as such.
