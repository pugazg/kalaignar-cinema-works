## Scene heading policy

- Only printed source-supported scene headings or clear dramatic transitions will become scene records.
- Page breaks alone will not create scenes.
- Story summary paragraphs will not become scenes.
- Embedded plays remain part of the parent film work and source order.
- Speaker labels will be preserved only after canonical transcription.
