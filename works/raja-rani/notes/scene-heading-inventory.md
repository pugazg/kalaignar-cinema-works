## Scene-separator audit

The source uses recurring star/ornament forms as dramatic boundary evidence, but typography varies.

Observed forms include:

- centered rows of stars;
- rule–star–rule ornaments such as `—★—`;
- standalone `★`;
- circular ornaments;
- star-flanked headings such as `★ தோட்டம் ★`.

Important examples:

- PDF 10 / printed p.9: centered star ornaments separate dramatic blocks;
- PDF 25 / printed p.24: rule–star–rule divider;
- PDF 31, 38, 42, 43, 46, 52, 53, 56, 58 and 59: recurring separator family retained in canonical transcription;
- PDF 66: parent-film action moves through `★ ராணி வீடு ★` and then into `★ சாக்ரடீஸ் (நாடகம்) ★`;
- PDF 73: source-visible divider marks the return from the Socrates performance to `(மனப் போராட்டம்)`;
- PDF 79 / printed p.78: `★ தோட்டம் ★` plus the final closing ornament.

A decorative star and a heading decoration are not automatically the same structural type. Each source-visible form must be interpreted in context.
