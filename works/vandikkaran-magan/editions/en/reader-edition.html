Archive scene 1 / source scene ID 1

ஜமீன்மாளிகை வெளிப்புறம்

Exterior of the zamindar’s palace.

(A crowd of townspeople has gathered... They are talking among themselves in a constant murmur.... With a loud cry of “Silence!”, Kalingarayan opens the door and comes out...)

மக்கள்Hey, Kalingarayar has arrived! The Zamindar will be here soon!

(Everyone looks toward the entrance—the zamindar arrives.)

ஒருவன்Zamindar Jambuling Bhoopathi!

மக்கள்Long live!

காளிங்What is this wild crowd—“long live, long live, long live the sword” and all that! Properly, nicely, in keeping with our shastras and customs, will you shout, “Jai! Jai! to Zamindar Jambuling Bhoopathi”?

ஒருவன்To Zamindar Jambuling Bhoopathi...

மக்கள்Jai!

ஜமீன்தார்Namaskaram to the elders! Blessings to the young!... Kalingaraya!...

காளிங்My lord!

ஜமீன்தார்Hear their grievances!

காளிங்As you command, my lord...

(A woman hands a petition to the zamindar—he receives it kindly—and the woman falls at the zamindar’s feet in reverence.)

ஜமீன்தார்Get up... get up... All of you fall at my feet and worship me; what am I—a saint? A maharishi? A mahatma?... No! But one thing: you are not bowing at the feet of Zamindar Jambulingam!... You are touching and worshipping the feet of dharma! The feet of justice—the feet of integrity! What do you say, Kalingaraya?... Am I right?..

காளிங்Who else can say it? We have to say it ourselves!

ஜமீன்தார்(to the woman who fell at his feet) What is your grievance, dear?

பெண்My husband drinks arrack all the time... he has brought the family to ruin!

ஜமீன்தார்Alas! Alas! Should womankind shed tears? And that too before my eyes? I simply cannot bear the tears of womankind... Kalinga!... Summon this lady’s husband and give him the proper order!... (to the woman) Don’t worry, mother!... It is our responsibility to reform your husband... Kalinga! One more order—and it must take effect immediately. Not even the smell of liquor should be allowed within the boundaries of our estate.

காளிங்Only within our boundaries, right!... We can enforce that.

புலவர்Even if the rains fail, Pari’s generosity never fails!
Kumanan was ready even to give his head!
Karna gave away as a gift
all the righteous deeds he had done! As Pari—as Kumanan—
as Karna, long live the munificent lord who gives!...
Long live!....

ஜமீன்தார்Kalinga!: Who is that man?...

புலவர்A Tamil poet, sir!

காளிங்You can tell from the flattery itself!..

புலவர்Don’t say that about all poets! There are poets who stand firm... and there are poets who keep time to suit the occasion!..

ஜமீன்தார்Kalinga... don’t hurt the poet’s feelings! Poet! What do you want?

புலவர்Here... my wife died, leaving me with this girl, Poongodi!... I am struggling with a motherless child!..

ஜமீன்தார்(lifting the child) Oh, poor thing!... She is like a little golden doll... Child... Have you got a cold, dear?... (wipes her nose.)

(The people look on in amazement; the child is handed back to the poet.)

Poet! Don’t worry. Every month, money will be sent from the estate to raise and educate this child. Leave your address with Manager Kalingarayan before you go...

புலவர்Thank you, sir, thank you!....

(Petitions pile up with Kalinga.)

ஜமீன்தார்Kalinga! Is the carriage ready?.... Where is the coachman?....

காளிங்Hey, Sadaiya!... Sadaiya!..

(Sadaiyan runs in carrying Uma.)

சடையன்Master... the carriage is ready!..

உமாAppa! I’ll come too, Appa!

ஜமீன்தார்Uma! No, dear... go and play... Appa will go to the temple and come back!

காளிங்Children shouldn’t go to the temple!... Come, dear, come with me!

(The zamindar comes and sits in the carriage. Sadaiyan climbs onto the carriage and drives—the carriage departs.)

தேவாலய அந்தரங்க அறை.

Private room of the temple.

(The zamindar enters... an opulent room... On the cot, Maragatham lies turned the other way, in decorative attire, with a strikingly alluring appearance...)

ஜமீன்தார்(in a coaxing voice toward the cot) Maragatham!... Asleep? Ah!... Even the beauty of you lying turned away is endlessly delightful!.. (going closer) Is it sleep? Sulking?... I’ll die without seeing your radiant face!... My sugarcane vine! Turn this way a little!....

(Maragatham turns and sits up....)

மரகதம்Do you know how long I’ve been waiting?...

ஜமீன்தார்I too flew here yearning to embrace my lovely peahen... but meanwhile, as usual, a herd of donkeys with mercy petitions at the estate palace gate!..

மரகதம்Couldn’t you have chased them away and come quickly?... I’m burning here with the torment of separation—go away!

(Comes forward to touch.)

ஜமீன்தார்(drawing away) Maragatham! Don’t touch my hand! Pour a little “Dettol” on my hand!... (going to the washbasin) Some poet—and I wiped his child’s nose... damn it!..

(Maragatham pours Dettol; the zamindar washes his hands. Maragatham gives him a towel and he wipes them...)

மரகதம்Then whose grievances did you settle today?....

ஜமீன்தார்An old woman gets a monthly allowance of one hundred rupees for as long as she lives.

மரகதம்That much money!...

ஜமீன்தார்That old woman won’t live even one more month!..

மரகதம்Hmm... then... any new order...

ஜமீன்தார்I’ve issued a new order that from now on not even the smell of liquor should enter our estate!..

மரகதம்Oh!.. (As she says this, she turns the bookcase—the cabinet of liquor bottles swings into view. Maragatham pours some into a glass and gives it to the zamindar...)

ஜமீன்தார்Maragatham... you too, a little... (He places the glass at her lips. She drinks. As the zamindar drinks from the same glass) The taste of liquor and the taste of Maragatham’s lips—both together give me a double intoxication... Ah!..

மரகதம்You yourself are a double man, aren’t you!...

ஜமீன்தார்So that was a jab... are you making fun of me?..

மரகதம்I said you alone have the strength of two men... isn’t that right, my beloved?....

ஜமீன்தார்Maragatham! You’re speaking from your own experience, aren’t you?...

மரகதம்Go away!.. (Shyly she lies back on the bed. The zamindar follows and embraces her..)

ஜமீன்தார்Maragatham... where is it?.. (He puckers his lips, asking for a kiss.) Give me one...

மரகதம்Uh-uh... I’m shy!..

ஜமீன்தார்What’s this sudden new shyness! There’s no one here—just the two of us... give it!...

மரகதம்There’s a third person too!..

ஜமீன்தார்A third person? What are you saying?

மரகதம்Watching from inside my belly..?

ஜமீன்தார்(startled) What? Pregnant?...

மரகதம்Yes... four months!..

ஜமீன்தார்Four months? Maragatham!... What foolishness is this? Why haven’t you got rid of it until now? You’re only telling me now?... Are you planning to claim a quarter share of the estate property?

மரகதம்Who wants your property? I don’t need it!... I want a child!

ஜமீன்தார்What did you say? Only if I leave you alive can you have children and all that... I’ll send you where you belong right now!...

(He grips Maragatham by the throat and throws her onto the bed. Maragatham, fallen on the bed, takes the revolver from beneath the pillow, first fires upward, and then aims at the zamindar.)

மரகதம்Mr. Jambuling Bhoopathi!... Until now Maragatham was a fawn that leapt and played in your lap!... From now on, a tiger cub that will drink your life!...

ஜமீன்தார்(frightened and shaken) Maragatham, forgive me!... Let us come to an understanding between ourselves..?

மரகதம்From now on, neither I nor the child I’m going to bear will live here! We will live in the estate palace!... That is where we are going to live—

ஜமீன்தார்All right, Maragatham!... In the estate palace, you’ll be the teacher who gives lessons to my daughter Uma!... We can live that way!..

(Just as Lingan’s idealistic song ends, the carriage driven by Uma comes racing toward him at great speed. Lingan snatches the whip from Uma’s hand.)

உமா(angrily) Hey! What’s wrong with you!

லிங்கன்Shall I give you the whip? Or shall I give you a whipping?

மரகதம்Little brother! Speak with a little respect! Whoever one may be, guard the tongue!..
