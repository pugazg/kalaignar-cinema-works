## காட்சி—1

*Archive scene 1 / source scene ID `1`*

**Source location:** ஜமீன்மாளிகை வெளிப்புறம்

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u001; source:PDF 6 / printed 5; source-scene:1 -->
*Exterior of the zamindar’s palace.*

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u002; source:PDF 6 / printed 5; source-scene:1 -->
*(A crowd of townspeople has gathered... They are talking among themselves in a constant murmur.... With a loud cry of “Silence!”, Kalingarayan opens the door and comes out...)*

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u003; source:PDF 6 / printed 5; source-scene:1 -->
**மக்கள்**  
Hey, Kalingarayar has arrived! The Zamindar will be here soon!

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u004; source:PDF 6 / printed 5; source-scene:1 -->
*(Everyone looks toward the entrance—the zamindar arrives.)*

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u005; source:PDF 6 / printed 5; source-scene:1 -->
**ஒருவன்**  
Zamindar Jambuling Bhoopathi!

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u006; source:PDF 6 / printed 5; source-scene:1 -->
**மக்கள்**  
Long live!

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u007; source:PDF 6 / printed 5; source-scene:1 -->
**காளிங்**  
What is this wild crowd—“long live, long live, long live the sword” and all that! Properly, nicely, in keeping with our shastras and customs, will you shout, “Jai! Jai! to Zamindar Jambuling Bhoopathi”?

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u008; source:PDF 6 / printed 5; source-scene:1 -->
**ஒருவன்**  
To Zamindar Jambuling Bhoopathi...

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u009; source:PDF 6 / printed 5; source-scene:1 -->
**மக்கள்**  
Jai!

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u010; source:PDF 6 / printed 5; source-scene:1 -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
Namaskaram to the elders! Blessings to the young!... Kalingaraya!...

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u011; source:PDF 6 / printed 5; source-scene:1 -->
**காளிங்**  
My lord!

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u012; source:PDF 6 / printed 5; source-scene:1 -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
Hear their grievances!

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u013; source:PDF 6 / printed 5; source-scene:1 -->
**காளிங்**  
As you command, my lord...

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u014; source:PDF 7 / printed 6; source-scene:1 -->
*(A woman hands a petition to the zamindar—he receives it kindly—and the woman falls at the zamindar’s feet in reverence.)*

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u015; source:PDF 7 / printed 6; source-scene:1 -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
Get up... get up... All of you fall at my feet and worship me; what am I—a saint? A maharishi? A mahatma?... No! But one thing: you are not bowing at the feet of Zamindar Jambulingam!... You are touching and worshipping the feet of dharma! The feet of justice—the feet of integrity! What do you say, Kalingaraya?... Am I right?..

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u016; source:PDF 7 / printed 6; source-scene:1 -->
**காளிங்**  
Who else can say it? We have to say it ourselves!

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u017; source:PDF 7 / printed 6; source-scene:1 -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
(to the woman who fell at his feet) What is your grievance, dear?

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u018; source:PDF 7 / printed 6; source-scene:1 -->
**பெண்**  
My husband drinks arrack all the time... he has brought the family to ruin!

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u019; source:PDF 7 / printed 6; source-scene:1 -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
Alas! Alas! Should womankind shed tears? And that too before my eyes? I simply cannot bear the tears of womankind... Kalinga!... Summon this lady’s husband and give him the proper order!... (to the woman) Don’t worry, mother!... It is our responsibility to reform your husband... Kalinga! One more order—and it must take effect immediately. Not even the smell of liquor should be allowed within the boundaries of our estate.

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u020; source:PDF 7 / printed 6; source-scene:1 -->
**காளிங்**  
Only within our boundaries, right!... We can enforce that.

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u021; source:PDF 7 / printed 6; source-scene:1 -->
**புலவர்**  
Even if the rains fail, Pari’s generosity never fails!
Kumanan was ready even to give his head!
Karna gave away as a gift
all the righteous deeds he had done! As Pari—as Kumanan—
as Karna, long live the munificent lord who gives!...
Long live!....

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u022; source:PDF 7 / printed 6; source-scene:1 -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
Kalinga!: Who is that man?...

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u023; source:PDF 7 / printed 6; source-scene:1 -->
**புலவர்**  
A Tamil poet, sir!

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u024; source:PDF 7 / printed 6; source-scene:1 -->
**காளிங்**  
You can tell from the flattery itself!..

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u025; source:PDF 7 / printed 6; source-scene:1 -->
**புலவர்**  
Don’t say that about all poets! There are poets who stand firm... and there are poets who keep time to suit the occasion!..

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u026; source:PDF 7 / printed 6; source-scene:1 -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
Kalinga... don’t hurt the poet’s feelings! Poet! What do you want?

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u027; source:PDF 8 / printed 7; source-scene:1 -->
**புலவர்**  
Here... my wife died, leaving me with this girl, Poongodi!... I am struggling with a motherless child!..

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u028; source:PDF 8 / printed 7; source-scene:1 -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
(lifting the child) Oh, poor thing!... She is like a little golden doll... Child... Have you got a cold, dear?... (wipes her nose.)

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u029; source:PDF 8 / printed 7; source-scene:1 -->
*(The people look on in amazement; the child is handed back to the poet.)*

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u030; source:PDF 8 / printed 7; source-scene:1 -->
Poet! Don’t worry. Every month, money will be sent from the estate to raise and educate this child. Leave your address with Manager Kalingarayan before you go...

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u031; source:PDF 8 / printed 7; source-scene:1 -->
**புலவர்**  
Thank you, sir, thank you!....

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u032; source:PDF 8 / printed 7; source-scene:1 -->
*(Petitions pile up with Kalinga.)*

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u033; source:PDF 8 / printed 7; source-scene:1 -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
Kalinga! Is the carriage ready?.... Where is the coachman?....

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u034; source:PDF 8 / printed 7; source-scene:1 -->
**காளிங்**  
Hey, Sadaiya!... Sadaiya!..

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u035; source:PDF 8 / printed 7; source-scene:1 -->
*(Sadaiyan runs in carrying Uma.)*

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u036; source:PDF 8 / printed 7; source-scene:1 -->
**சடையன்**  
Master... the carriage is ready!..

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u037; source:PDF 8 / printed 7; source-scene:1 -->
**உமா**  
Appa! I’ll come too, Appa!

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u038; source:PDF 8 / printed 7; source-scene:1 -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
Uma! No, dear... go and play... Appa will go to the temple and come back!

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u039; source:PDF 8 / printed 7; source-scene:1 -->
**காளிங்**  
Children shouldn’t go to the temple!... Come, dear, come with me!

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s001-u040; source:PDF 8 / printed 7; source-scene:1 -->
*(The zamindar comes and sits in the carriage. Sadaiyan climbs onto the carriage and drives—the carriage departs.)*

## காட்சி — 4

*Archive scene 4 / source scene ID `4`*

**Source location:** ஜமீன் தேவாலயம் உட்புறம்

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s004-u001; source:PDF 9 / printed 8; source-scene:4 -->
*Interior of the estate temple.*

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s004-u002; source:PDF 9 / printed 8; source-scene:4 -->
*(The goddess idol is decorated. Behind the pedestal on which the idol stands is what looks like a partition wall. The zamindar enters, removes his footwear by the doorway, ties the silk cloth draped over him around his waist, breaks the coconut, places the fruit before the idol, lights the camphor and waves it before the idol...)*

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s004-u003; source:PDF 9 / printed 8; source-scene:4 -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
Devi! Parasakthi! Jagan Mata!.. Om Sakthi! Om Sakthi! Om! Om Sakthi, Om Sakthi, Om Sakthi, Om!...

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s004-u004; source:PDF 9 / printed 8; source-scene:4 -->
*(He stands with his eyes closed in meditation, touches his cheeks, opens his eyes and looks at Sadaiyan....)*

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s004-u005; source:PDF 9 / printed 8; source-scene:4 -->
Sadaiya!... Once I sit down for puja, I don’t know when I’ll get up... Go see to the other work and come back leisurely... go!...

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s004-u006; source:PDF 9 / printed 8; source-scene:4 -->
*(Sadaiyan bows to him and then to the goddess idol and leaves with great humility. The zamindar bolts the door. Then he turns the goddess idol. A secret door opens behind the idol, and through it the zamindar enters his private luxurious room.)*

<a id="archive-scene-5"></a>
## காட்சி — 4 எ.

*Archive scene 5 / source scene ID `4-எ`*

**Source location:** தேவாலய அந்தரங்க அறை.

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u001; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
*Private room of the temple.*

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u002; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
*(The zamindar enters... an opulent room... On the cot, Maragatham lies turned the other way, in decorative attire, with a strikingly alluring appearance...)*

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u003; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
(in a coaxing voice toward the cot) Maragatham!... Asleep? Ah!... Even the beauty of you lying turned away is endlessly delightful!.. (going closer) Is it sleep? Sulking?... I’ll die without seeing your radiant face!... My sugarcane vine! Turn this way a little!....

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u004; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
*(Maragatham turns and sits up....)*

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u005; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
**மரகதம்**  
Do you know how long I’ve been waiting?...

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u006; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
I too flew here yearning to embrace my lovely peahen... but meanwhile, as usual, a herd of donkeys with mercy petitions at the estate palace gate!..

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u007; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
**மரகதம்**  
Couldn’t you have chased them away and come quickly?... I’m burning here with the torment of separation—go away!

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u008; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
*(Comes forward to touch.)*

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u009; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
(drawing away) Maragatham! Don’t touch my hand! Pour a little “Dettol” on my hand!... (going to the washbasin) Some poet—and I wiped his child’s nose... damn it!..

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u010; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
*(Maragatham pours Dettol; the zamindar washes his hands. Maragatham gives him a towel and he wipes them...)*

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u011; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
**மரகதம்**  
Then whose grievances did you settle today?....

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u012; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
An old woman gets a monthly allowance of one hundred rupees for as long as she lives.

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u013; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
**மரகதம்**  
That much money!...

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u014; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
That old woman won’t live even one more month!..

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u015; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
**மரகதம்**  
Hmm... then... any new order...

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u016; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
I’ve issued a new order that from now on not even the smell of liquor should enter our estate!..

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u017; source:PDF 10 / printed 9; source-scene:4-எ -->
**மரகதம்**  
Oh!.. (As she says this, she turns the bookcase—the cabinet of liquor bottles swings into view. Maragatham pours some into a glass and gives it to the zamindar...)

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u018; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
Maragatham... you too, a little... (He places the glass at her lips. She drinks. As the zamindar drinks from the same glass) The taste of liquor and the taste of Maragatham’s lips—both together give me a double intoxication... Ah!..

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u019; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
**மரகதம்**  
You yourself are a double man, aren’t you!...

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u020; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
So that was a jab... are you making fun of me?..

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u021; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
**மரகதம்**  
I said you alone have the strength of two men... isn’t that right, my beloved?....

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u022; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
Maragatham! You’re speaking from your own experience, aren’t you?...

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u023; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
**மரகதம்**  
Go away!.. (Shyly she lies back on the bed. The zamindar follows and embraces her..)

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u024; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
Maragatham... where is it?.. (He puckers his lips, asking for a kiss.) Give me one...

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u025; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
**மரகதம்**  
Uh-uh... I’m shy!..

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u026; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
What’s this sudden new shyness! There’s no one here—just the two of us... give it!...

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u027; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
**மரகதம்**  
There’s a third person too!..

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u028; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
A third person? What are you saying?

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u029; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
**மரகதம்**  
Watching from inside my belly..?

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u030; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
(startled) What? Pregnant?...

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u031; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
**மரகதம்**  
Yes... four months!..

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u032; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
**ஜமீன்தார்**  
Four months? Maragatham!... What foolishness is this? Why haven’t you got rid of it until now? You’re only telling me now?... Are you planning to claim a quarter share of the estate property?

<!-- unit:vandikkaran-magan-en-s005-u033; source:PDF 11 / printed 10; source-scene:4-எ -->
