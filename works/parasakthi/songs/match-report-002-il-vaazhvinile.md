## Match classification

Status: **confirmed same underlying song; line-level comparison complete**.

Identity does not rest on the title alone. The two witnesses share the same
alternating turn structure, the same sequence of lines and the same refrain cue.

which is the anthology's opening form `இல் வாழ்வினிலே` — the booklet's own cue
therefore corroborates the anthology's title rather than contradicting it.

## Authorship disposition

The anthology's contents page (PDF 28) explicitly lists
`இல் வாழ்வினிலே ஒளி ஏற்றும் தீபம்` beside `கலைஞர்`. That is an item-level
witness, and it is stronger than the secondary soundtrack table that previously
supplied this record's lyricist.

The Parasakthi record therefore now reads `மு. கருணாநிதி` with
`evidence_basis: anthology-attributed`.

### Superseded evidence, preserved

The earlier attribution came from `tracklist-evidence.json` track 4
(`இல் வாழ்வினிலே`), which names **பாரதிதாசன்**. That witness is retained
unchanged in `tracklist-evidence.json`; it is no longer the controlling evidence
for this record, and the disagreement is recorded rather than erased.

### What this does not establish

`anthology-attributed` is not original-film `primary-source-verified`
authorship. The 2024 anthology is authoritative for what that edition prints and
attributes. Neither the Parasakthi booklet's own song-credits page — which lists
six contributors booklet-wide and pairs none with a song — nor this anthology
constitutes an original 1952 item-level credit for this song.
