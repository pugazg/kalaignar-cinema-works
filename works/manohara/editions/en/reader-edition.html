பத்மாVasantha Sena! If the very form of truth rises as a storm, you will sink into that ash-heap. Beware!

வசந்திYou dare threaten me?

பத்மாYes—you. A land where the sky pours rain! A forest where deer roam! A home where honey showers—our Chola realm. You came there bearing the kamandalam of love, devised plot after plot and plundered a beautiful life. I welcomed the woman who came and gave her space to live in pleasure all these days! And today you speak with arrogance: you to rule, we to perish. No! You yourself are performing the aarti for your own destruction. Do not perish in vain!

வசந்திWhat did you say?

[Vasanthi slaps Padmavathi.]

மனோMother!...... [He cries out.]

பத்மாManohara! [She goes to him.]

பத்Manohara! My unfading lamp of fame! Forgive me, da! Whenever you rose in valour to compose a heroic epic in defence of the greatness of the Chola Empire, it was I—the culprit—who hid the stylus, concealed the palm leaves and stood in your way. My gold, who bore your mother's command upon your head! Even if your own mother now commands you, ‘Destroy injustice,’ you have been brought to a state where you cannot move, my treasure! Ruined. Everything is ruined.

மனோNo, Mother......! No! My own mother—now give the order, the command: ‘Destroy injustice!’ The final hour! The final plea! My Tamil Mother! After seeing this outrage—the little jackal that entered this land through a pass opening amid the pleasure-grove, settled here, and today has laid the first token for climbing the throne—will you at least now grant permission? I need no sword, Mother! One spoken word is enough. I need no army, Mother—only let the mother's ban be lifted! Give me no blade, Mother—one glance of the eye is enough—and freedom for you, for me, for everyone. Where is the order, Mother? Give the order. I shall erase these people until no trace of their form remains.

பத்மாMy precious treasure! It was for your father's good that I bound your heroic hands. I bowed my head before this venomous woman. After the very man for whose sake I kept saying patience, patience has himself been locked in prison, where is the greatness left in patience? Manohara! Enough endurance. Rise in a surge!

மனோMother!

பத்The mother's command has been given. Set forth! Manohara! Manohara!! Manohara!! Manohara!!!

[She runs to the king's statue.]

பத்Maharaja! Do you see how our treasured family is being destroyed? Look at Manoharan, bound and writhing. Look at Manoharan, Maharaja! Maharaja!! Maharaja!!!

[Ugrasenan and Vasanthi laugh.]

பத்Why are you laughing? Monkeys leaping in a red-banana grove! Great sinners who came to bend this land, one in the form of a woman, one in the form of a holy man! You who forgot the history the world tells—that those who laughed so have not lived, and those who wept have not been ruined! Little pack of jackals who came by sleight of hand to break the sceptre of the flourishing Tamils! Do not laugh! Pack of villains unable to know the power of tears! Do not laugh, forgetting that tears have accomplished what even the point of a sword has not. Traitors who, amid the afflicted, compose sandhu and sing sindhu—do not laugh, do not laugh...... O soil of the motherland! Look at Manoharan, the sacred lamp of your house! Chola Mother! Look at the agony of the warrior of warriors who placed a victory-garland upon your beautiful shoulder. Look at the son who marked your lovely forehead with tilak from the blood of foreign enemies—alas!—now bound and suffering in the fetters of slavery. O ceremonial hall of the Chola family, how long will you make the form of my victory bow his head amid these useless men? A tender infant—the crown prince! A king who is to rule. O Mother Earth who bears the sinner thirsting to avenge herself upon them all! If it is true that you are grateful; if it is true that the tears of mothers born in the warrior clan possess power; if it is true that the great warrior Manoharan, who has never known how to kneel before an outsider, is my son—let these chains shatter into fragments, let the laughter of the unjust cease, let this very hall collapse into dust. Into dust! Into dust!! Into dust!!!

[By Manoharan's strength the pillar breaks and falls; the voice of motherhood too becomes his aid.]

உக்ரSeize him! Arrest him!

வசந்திStab him! Kill him.

[Ugrasenan is killed by Manoharan; the Pandya army also arrives. Kesari Varman saves the child.]

[The dagger Vasanthi throws at Manoharan strikes Vasanthan.]

வசந்திVasantha!

வசந்Anna!

மனோVasantha...! Vasantha...! Vasantha...!

வசந்Anna!

வசந்தன்Anna, will you not call me ‘thambi’ just once! Say it, Anna—say ‘thambi’!

மனோThambi!

வசAnna! If the dagger this sinner threw had struck you, I understand how much pain you would suffer. You must live, Anna! Anna, you must rule the country! I must die.

[Vasanthan dies. As Manoharan tries to kill Vasanthi...]

கேசரிNo, Prince! Leave her to me. I have waited many days for this good deed. She must not die in a single stroke. Until her final breath, she must be killed little... by little. [Vasanthi runs.]

கேசரிWhere are you running? Woman born to rule! Unruly shrew—stop! Stop!

[The prison door opens.]

அரசர்Satyaseelar! Forgive me. Where is Padmavathi? Where is my Padmavathi!

சத்யCome with me.

அரசர்Rajapriya! Rajam priya!
