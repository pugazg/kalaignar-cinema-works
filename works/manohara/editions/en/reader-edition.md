Let joy overflow in those eyes clouded by sorrow. Let the clouds of grief flee far from you. Troubles—henceforth, dust to us...... Ha... ha... ha... Victory; victory;

<!-- unit:manohara-en-s001-u005; source:PDF 7 / printed 6 -->

<!-- unit:manohara-en-s001-u006; source:PDF 7→8 / printed 6→7 -->
**கேசரி**

Your poison did not kill me, woman. Kesari Varman is alive! He has not died—he will not die, not before exposing your plot! Cheat!.....glib-tongued trickster!

<!-- unit:manohara-en-s001-u009; source:PDF 8 / printed 7 -->
**பெரிய**  
Ha ha ha............

<!-- unit:manohara-en-s001-u010; source:PDF 8 / printed 7 -->
**கேசரி**  
Guruji.

<!-- unit:manohara-en-s001-u011; source:PDF 8 / printed 7 -->
**பெரி**  
It's the statue, my boy!

<!-- unit:manohara-en-s001-u012; source:PDF 8 / printed 7 -->

A statue, yes! Why am I sculpting her? To rehearse the torture; Vasantha Sena! I will thrust a spear into your eyes—set your hair aflame—those kovvai-red lips!... I will drive sharp needles into them..... Your mirror-bright cheeks! Ha ha ha... I will slash them with a dagger... that body that drives onlookers mad... I will hack it into pieces and fling them away! The wondrous work of invisibility has begun, woman!... Enchantress, the chapter of vengeance by your former husband has begun. Walking serpent!... Dance of destruction itself! Treacherous demon... gnawing spider.........
