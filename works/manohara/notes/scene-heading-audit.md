# மனோகரா — scene-heading / structural-transition audit

Source: `TVA_BOK_0010102_மனோகரா.pdf`  
Source authority: rendered scan  
Audit status: **verified for intake/mapping**

## Disposition

The supplied booklet does **not** use numbered scene headings. This audit therefore does not invent `Scene 1`, `Scene 2`, etc.

Instead, the source signals changes through a mixture of:

- decorative star separators;
- parenthetical stage/location transitions;
- bracketed stage/location transitions;
- bare location lines;
- narrative transitions embedded between dialogue blocks;
- continuous dialogue/action with no explicit heading.

A transition marker is not automatically treated as a scene boundary. Later scene derivatives, if useful, must be designed only after verified canonical Tamil exists and must retain this source ambiguity rather than pretending that the booklet supplied a numbered scene system.

## Separator observations

Decorative stars occur repeatedly, sometimes as three spaced stars and sometimes with printing/OCR confusion in the machine layer. They are treated as structural separators only. They must **not** later be expanded into invented prose such as `(Scene ends.)`.

Several transitions occur without stars, and several stars occur without a clean location heading. That is the main reason a numeric scene count is not source-supported at intake.
