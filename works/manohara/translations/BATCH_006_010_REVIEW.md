### Scene 7 — Manoharan's moral and martial rhetoric

### Scene 8 — play-within-the-play and empty speaker fields

Scene 8 is the batch's largest record: **77 verified English units**. All **58/58** immutable labelled dialogue records are linked exactly once.

The English therefore translates the prologue as source-unlabelled speech with `speaker_label: null`; it does not manufacture a Kesari dialogue-record link. The same rule is applied to the empty-speaker continuations after Eeswari Varman, Ajayan and Kamalavathi, Vikatan's audience reaction, and the final accusation voice.

The nested-play identities remain distinct from the outer story. The exact source label `வர்மா` in `manohara-s008-d003` remains unresolved in metadata and is not normalized by translation.

The source-visible love-song reference `நிலாவிலே ! சல்லாபமே!!` is represented as `manohara-en-s008-u050`, linked to **`manohara-song-002`**. Only the printed title/refrain reference is translated; no missing lyrics are supplied.

### Scene 9 — Vasantha Sena's performance and Manoharan's alliteration

The source-unlabelled `(தனக்குள்)` monologue and the unfinished two-cup quotation remain direct source-linked English units without invented speaker metadata. Vasantha Sena's renunciation performance retains its ornaments / luxury / royal pleasure / saffron robe / begging-bowl accumulation.

The verified rendering preserves the underlying festering-wound / civet-perfume / false-mask images and accelerating threat rather than replacing the passage with a plain summary.

### Scene 10 — historical/play parallel and martial wordplay
