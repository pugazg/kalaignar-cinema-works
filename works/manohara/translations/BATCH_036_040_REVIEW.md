## Scene-specific decisions

### Scene 36 — the central court confrontation

This scene contains **88 immutable labelled dialogue records** and is preserved as the rhetorical centre of the work rather than condensed into modern prose. Manoharan's childhood-memory reproach retains the sequence of gold, gems, diamonds, `கண்ணே / முத்தே / தமிழ்ப் பண்ணே`, ivory bed and sandalwood cradle before turning the king's `கட்டளை` into an accusation of unappeased desire.

The court challenge preserves the murder / robbery / `குள்ள நரி` questions and the gathering of scholars, ministers, warrior-clan heroes, people's representatives and the country's pillars. The Parasurama exchange remains the source's abrupt contrast: **`Parasurama—an avatar. Manoharan—a human being!`**

Three genuine cross-page source utterances remain single English units with physical page segments:

- `manohara-en-s036-u035` / `manohara-s036-d032`, PDF **51→52**;
- `manohara-en-s036-u044` / `manohara-s036-d041`, PDF **52→53**;
- `manohara-en-s036-u080` / `manohara-s036-d077`, PDF **56→57**.

The long `d041` speech is not summarized. It retains the rolling-eyes accusation, warrior-king lineage, peacock/monkey contrast, cool-moon/firebrand image, Padmavathi as birthplace of love / abode of righteousness / form of compassion / embodiment of chastity / flawless gem / undiminished gold, and the repeated command to run. **Purananuru**, **Kalingathu Parani** and **abhishekam** remain culturally explicit rather than being domesticated away.

Padmavathi's later speeches preserve their bitter reversals: first stab the womb from which Manoharan was born; better death than apology; the rare `royal fortune` in which father kills son and mother nods; and the final-kiss / graves image. Vijaya's rose, jasmine, suffering-waves, desert, dark-room and sorrow-lotus imagery likewise remains visible.

The reciprocal `வேசி` insults on PDF 57 are translated directly rather than softened. Source-attached actions—chain breaking, Padmavathi's arrival, the guard's entrance and other cues stored inside immutable records—remain inside their linked English units.

### Scene 37 — escape and reason to live

The invisible rescue remains unexplained until the source explains it. Manoharan's repeated request to be killed is retained against Rajapriyan's reversal: obedience to the mother's word cannot mean abandoning that same imprisoned mother, and Vijaya is imprisoned too.

### Scene 38 — prison, beauty satire and the mock durbar

The prison's pregnancy / gruel exchange remains distinct from Vasantha Sena's fruit-juice vanity conversation and from Vasanthan's comic mock durbar. The donkey-milk / tiger-milk escalation and Vasantha Sena's boast that she is the king's **Veda-book** are preserved.

The mock royal honorific chain is deliberately allowed to swell before collapsing into `உப்பில்லாம கலக்கஞ்சி குடிக்கும் வசந்த மகா ராஜா பராக்! பராக்!`. `பராக்` remains **paraak**, and Vasanthan's parroted replies stay repetitive. One source line begins only with `:`—`இப்ப நான் சொல்றபடி சொல்லணும்......`—and is therefore `manohara-en-s038-u023` with `speaker_label: null`; no inferred speaker is assigned.

### Scene 39 — seduction, coronation proposal and invisibility

The Ahalya / Indra, Tara / Chandra, Vasantha / Purushothaman sequence remains explicit. The king's `முதலில் நீ பைத்தியம்—பிறகு நான் பைத்தியம்—அப்புறம் இந்த நாடே பைத்தியம்` retains its three-step escalation. Vasantha Sena's `ஞான சூன்யம்` remains close to the source as **void of wisdom**.

The source explicitly labels her internal monologue `வசந்தசேனை மனதிற்குள்`; translation keeps it linked to that exact label instead of converting it into external speech. The invisibility medicine remains continuous with the earlier guru material, and no identity is inferred beyond the printed `அரூபம் / அரூ` labels.

### Scene 40 — intercepted letter and strategy

The complete printed letter on PDF **65** is preserved as one `written-text` unit with salutation, body and signature. No dialogue speaker is invented for it. Kesari Varman's `ஓலை / சாவோலை` contrast is retained as **letter / death-letter**, while the final `முள்ளை முள் கொண்டு எடுப்பது போல` is translated literally: **just as a thorn is removed with another thorn, a plot must be defeated by a plot**.

The strategic distinction remains central: merely killing Vasantha Sena would display Manoharan's valour, but would not expose her disguise and schemes to the king or the world.
