- the triple `கன்னம் / கிண்ணம் / வண்ணம் / சின்னம்` repetition remains visibly repetitive rather than becoming a compact English refrain;

- the paired **two fish / two honeys / two deer / two skies** sequence remains line-by-line imagery rather than being decoded into body-part explanation.

### 034 — `காகித ஓடம் கடலலை மீது`

- the three-person **paper boat** and shared sinking image is retained without sentimental rewriting;
- kolam, flags, chariot, fire, season and hour remain separate source images;
- the accusation that there is nobody to hear crying, nobody to console, nowhere for the poor to live and no god in any temple remains uncompressed;
- `ஆறிலும் சாவு நூறிலும் சாவு` remains literally **death at six, death at a hundred**, rather than being replaced by a generalized proverb;
- the final call remains **Amma... O mother**.

### 035 — `ஒண்ணு கொடுத்தா ஒன்பது கிடைக்கும்`

- cheek/picture, fruit-lip seal, marriage and `எண்ணிக்கொள் / பண்ணிக்கொள்` verbal pairing remain visible;
- the second half preserves bathing, memory, love putting forth shoots, giving what was asked, the home, throbbing `பருவம்` and the restraining heart.

### 036 — `நெஞ்சுக்கு நீதியும்`

- `நெஞ்சுக்கு நீதியும் / தோளுக்கு வாளும்` remains **justice for the heart / sword for the shoulder**;
- the compact radiant-gem/cotton/fire opening is retained conservatively rather than rebuilt into a different polished stanza;
- Bharathi, Buddha, Gandhi and Arignar Anna remain named in the same rhetorical chain;
- `சாதி மதங்களை சாய்ப்போம்` remains the direct **bring down caste and religions** formulation;
- duty, rights, folly and living as human beings remain explicit;
- `அண்ணே` / `அப்பா` remain audible as **anne / appa**;
- the final corruption/mirror/blame/pillar warning remains accusatory rather than neutralized.

### 037 — `குடி உயர கோல் உயரும்`

The complete two-page social-political satire remains one record, PDF **90–91**.

- `குடி / கோல்` remains **people / sceptre**, preserving the source's political reversal;
- people **cooking in burning fire** remains deliberately harsh;
- the king-in-mud-house / kings-on-the-road satire remains direct;
- verified `சிறுகொடிந்த` is retained as **sirukodindha** rather than silently corrected;
- `பங்களா`, `புளிச்சக்காடி`, car/light and move-aside sleeping imagery remain source-shaped;
- pearls/nose-mucus, blood-wound/gem, stars/diamonds and rainbow/`vaiduriyam` inversions remain explicit;
- `குத்துக் கல்லு` remains **kuthuk-kallu** as a documented source pressure point;
- `செத்துப் பிழைக்கும்` remains **survive by dying**, preserving the closing social violence.

### 038 — `ஆயிரம் பிறைகள் காணும் வரை`

- `தமிழே` remains direct address as **O Tamil**;

### 039 — `குறிஞ்சி மலரொன்று குலுங்கி அழைக்குது`

- `அணைத்தால் விளக்கும் இரண்டு விடும்` remains **if embraced, the two lamps will go out**, preserving the source's embrace/extinguish pressure without inventing a body-part gloss;
