## Song-by-song voice dispositions

### 012 — `அம்மையப்பா அருள்புரிவாய் எல்லாம் உன் செயல்`

The English preserves the song's unusual combination of devotion, paradox, social hierarchy and proverbial imagery.

- `அம்மையப்பா`, `ஐயன்`, `அப்பா` remain audible rather than collapsing into one generic `Lord`;
- this life/afterlife, pleasure/pain, truth/falsehood and worldly bonds remain a paired accumulation;
- honey/poison, clear nectar, honey-cascade, fire-forest, burning pool and volcanoes remain concrete contradictions;
- `அரசன் முதல் ஆண்டிகள்` retains the king-to-`aandis` social span instead of being rewritten as generalized equality;
- palanquin-bearers and those riding in procession remain separate source images;
- the toad within stone, oil within sesame and venom within the snake's fang remain intact.

### 013 — `நீலக்கடல் பாரு பாப்பா...`

The child-address remains simple, repetitive and affectionate.

- repeated `பாப்பா` is retained as **paappa** rather than varied into multiple English pet names;
- blue sea, silver-sand bed, running, leaping and strolling remain literal play images;
- `சந்தனக் கிளி`, peacock and `அமுதப் புறா` remain **sandalwood parrot**, **beautiful peacock**, and **nectar-dove**;
- repeated `சாய்ந்தாடு` remains repeated **sway**;
- the final `(சா)` remains an unexpanded source refrain cue.

### 014 — `காதல் துறையே புதுமைக் கனவே`

The duet keeps its dense romantic image-chain and exact turn structure.

- `காதல் துறை` remains **realm of love**;
- `ஓடிவா` remains **come running** rather than a softened `come to me`;
- great cloud / grove-plumage imagery remains visible;
- great gem, treasure of blissful life, maiden dancing in the eyes, boundary of bliss and heart-veena remain separate images;
- `வீணை` remains **veena**;
- the final source variation from love-realm to **moon of love** is preserved rather than forcing a uniform refrain;
- exact `முத்தாயி`, `முத்தன்`, `இருவரும்` labels remain source metadata, with no performer inference.

### 015 — `காதல் துறையே புதுமைக் கனவே — சோக கீதம்`

This sorrow-version deliberately echoes song 014 where the Tamil echoes it.

- repeated source lines retain the same English wording rather than being stylistically rewritten;
- cloud/plumage and great-gem imagery remain linked to the preceding duet;
- `தென்றலில் வீசும் கானமே` remains a **song that drifts upon the breeze**;
- `தேன்மழை` remains **honey-rain**;
- the final source does not identify whose lap is meant, so the English does not invent a possessor.

### 016 — `சின்னப் புது மலரே`

This song is a major Kalaignar-language density test.

- `அகல் விளக்கு` remains **agal-lamp**;
- flower, sugarcane taste, rock-candy, fruit-nectar, red-paddy ear, red sugarcane and flower-of-Tamil remain concrete accumulations;
- `செந்தமிழ்`, Kaveri and `திராவிட` remain explicitly Tamil/Dravidian rather than being generalized into regional praise;
- `கற்றார்ப் பெட்டகம்` remains a **casket of the learned**;
- the verified difficult lines `நீ... இராவிடம் இருள் சூழும் / என்ற நிலை தந்த` are **not silently repaired**. The English preserves the visible wording and records the syntactic pressure point;
- source-split `விண்ண முதே` is translated through its heavenly-nectar sense without altering the Tamil witness.

### 017 — `சிதைந்ததே என் காதல் உயிரோவியம்`

The grief remains architectural and pictorial rather than generic heartbreak.

- `உயிரோவியம்` remains **living portrait**;
- `சுவைக் காவியம்` remains the unusual **epic of flavour** rather than being softened to `beautiful story`;
- life/flame, storm/flower-garden, divine statue and empty-temple images remain distinct;
- `வெறுங் கோவிலானதே என் வாழ்வுதான்` remains **my life has become an empty temple**;
- repeated `(மன)` cues remain unexpanded.

### 018 — `காதல் புறா காதிலே பேசுவதென்னோ`

The lament keeps its recurring animal, body, landscape and light imagery.

- the **love-dove speaking into the ear** remains literal;
- repeated `பாசம் கொண்டதும் வீணோ? / பாவை ஏன் பிறந்தேனோ?` remains repeated questioning;
- `தளிர்மேனி` remains a **tender-shoot body** rather than merely `delicate body`;
- repeated `வா... வா...` remains repeated **come... come...**;
- the verified spaced source phrase `மான் தோல் வேங்கை` is retained as the unsettling **deer-skinned tiger** image rather than silently normalized;
- `மாங்குயில்` remains **mango-cuckoo**;
- darkness where sorrow dwells and the sleeping moon-maiden scattering light remain concrete;
- love leading to deception is kept direct rather than softened to disappointment.
