## Song-by-song voice dispositions

### 019 — `வாங்க... வாங்க... வாங்க...`

The two-page theatre-ticket number remains noisy, colloquial and joke-driven rather than becoming polished musical-theatre prose.

- repeated `வாங்க` remains repeated **come**;
- the floor/stain sequence retains floor, blood-stain and betel/areca-stain images rather than inventing a new English rhyme for `தரை / கறை`;
- `ட்ராமா / வெங்கட்ராமா` remains audible through the Venkatrama name;
- `காலி / நாற்காலி` is retained as `kaali / naarkaali` because the joke is sound-based;
- `ஆடல் / பாடல் / ஊடல் / கூடல்` remains a four-part dance/song/sulking/reunion sequence;
- full charge, double charge, dressing-scene torch and house-full theatre patter remain direct;
- the `பீரியா / பீரியோ` misunderstanding and the source's heavily sound-driven final chained forms are transliterated rather than rewritten into a different joke.

The translation therefore keeps Kalaignar's stage-comedy rhythm even where the English necessarily carries Tamil sound fragments.

### 020 — `வேலை யில்லாத தொல்லை`

The social reversal remains explicit:

- unemployment/no-work is not generalized into mere hardship;
- the trouble vanishes like morning dew;
- unexpected good life arrives on its own;
- strangers becoming kin and sorrow leaving remain separate lines;
- `சீமாட்டி` is retained as **seemaatti** to keep the social-rank joke in the final mistaken-identity turn.

### 021 — `ஆழிசூழ் உலகம் விடிந்த தென்று என்`

The accusatory imagery remains severe and source-shaped:

- `ஆழிசூழ் உலகம்` remains **ocean-girdled world**;
- rooster-crowing and pecking away chastity remain literal;
- `ஊழி` keeps its end-age/catastrophic force;
- `தாழி` remains a **thaazhi-vessel**, not a generic abstraction;
- `கற்புக்கு இலக்கணம்` remains the remarkable **grammar of chastity** formulation;
- repeated `சொல்லுமோ / நில்லுமோ` questioning remains repetition rather than summary.

### 022 — `மணிப் புறா!... புது மணிப் புறா!...`

The intimate lyric retains its concrete invitation imagery:

- `மணிப் புறா` remains **jewel-dove**;
- the heart still leaps toward the beloved;
- the bed still spreads flowers and calls;
- `பூமானே` is retained as **Poomaane** rather than confidently replacing a compact source form with a guessed equivalent;
- rose enclosure / jasmine garden remains attached to the speaker's face;
- Raja/Rani and pleasure language remains direct rather than euphemized.

### 023 — `கண்ணற்ற தகப்பனுக்கு பெண்ணாகப் பிறந்தவளே!`

This two-page record intentionally preserves the anthology's mixture of song and spoken dialogue.

- `வீணா / வாலி / போலி` remains a sound-chain through transliteration;
- cat-closing-eyes/world-dark and curtain/moon ridicule remains literal;
- `கட்டிக் கரும்பு` is rendered conservatively as **bundled sugarcane** and documented as a comic pressure point;
- Harishchandra and the sarcastic `அநியாயமாய் உண்மை` turn remain together;
- `வசனம்` and `பாட்டு` divisions are retained rather than extracting only sung lines;
- card language remains explicit: queen in a deck, Clavar Queen, Daiman Queen and Ispade Queen preserve the source spellings rather than silently modernizing the suit names;
- peahen, flower-cuckoo, truth/sin and yearning images/questions remain intact.

### 024 — `பொதுநலம் என்றும் பொதுநலம்`

The public-welfare song preserves its political-social imagery and extended medicinal metaphor.

- `புகழ் உடல்` remains **body of fame**;
- household/kin images expand into country and collective sacrifice without being compressed into generic patriotism;
- the idle man's body-as-cage and useless-man ridicule remain direct;
- `பண்டாரம்`, `பரதேசி`, `கோவிந்தா` retain culture-bearing force;
- `நடைபிணம்` remains **walking corpse**;
- forehead lines, old-age lines and harms growing in the mind remain separate;
- `அறிவுக்கோயில்` remains **temple of knowledge**;
- `உழைக்கும் தோழர்` remains **working comrade**;
- the medicine that gives light to higher duties closes the same metaphor begun earlier.

### 025 — `ஆயர்பாடி கண்ணா நீ`

The lyric's devotional/flirtatious register remains intact.

- `Ayarpadi Kanna`, `Mayakkara`, `Jaalakkara` and `Bhagavane` remain culturally audible rather than being flattened into generic divine titles;
- repeated `வா வா வா` and invocations remain repeated;
- Bhama remains named;
- unbearable love still blows like burning embers;
- the body still sprinkles love;
- sky-racing lightning becomes the waist;
- the cheek still seeps honey and becomes a divine drink;
- `வேலைப் பழிக்கும் விழி` remains eyes that **put the spear to shame**.
