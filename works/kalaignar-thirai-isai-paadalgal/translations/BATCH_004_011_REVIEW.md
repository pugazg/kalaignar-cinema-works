## Song-by-song voice dispositions

### 004 — `மாரி மகமாயி மாரி`

The English keeps the song's mixed register rather than reducing it to devotional verse:

- `மாரி மகமாயி`, `ஆயி`, `காளியம்மா`, `மும்மாரி`, `கொடும்பாவி`, `திரி சூலி`, `நீலி`, and `ஓங்கார ரூபிணி` retain cultural/transliterated force where flattening them would erase the source register;
- the water → cloud → rain reasoning remains explicit;
- the colloquial woman-speaker passage stays conversational;
- the line `வரி கொடுக்க முடியாமல் / வாடுறோம் தாயே` remains the blunt tax grievance embedded in the devotional appeal;
- the unmatched opening parenthesis in the male passage is not silently repaired.

### 005 — `பேசும் யாழே பெண் மானே`

The romantic duet keeps its image density:

- `யாழ்` remains **yaazh**;
- sky/moon, Tamil epic, unpainted painting and fruit-nectar images remain concrete;
- `பேசி பேசி / வீசி வீசி` remains doubled;
- `நாதம் / பேதம்` is retained as a sound/resonance versus difference/division turn rather than replaced by an invented English rhyme;
- `மாதர் மறவர்` remains **women and warriors** rather than a generic lovers phrase.

### 006 — `மணமில்லா மலர் நானம்மா!`

The central `மணம்` wordplay is made visible as **fragrance / marriage** instead of choosing one sense and losing the other.

The broken veena, thunderbolt, flower-garden, vine, cyclone, pollen, dark house, storm-wave and sorrow-grove images are retained line by line. The source page has no separate singer credit, and the English does not infer one.

### 007 — `பேசும் யாழே பெண் மானே (சோகம்)`

This lament deliberately echoes song 005 but turns the same yaazh image toward grief.

- `மீளா சோகம் மீட்டிடும் யாழானேன்` keeps the musical action in `மீட்டிடும்` as **a yaazh that plucks an unreturning sorrow**;
- the **singing ghost** image is not softened;
- `தீரா துன்பம் தீட்டிய ஏடானேன்` remains an inscribed-page image;
- all three `(பேசும்)` refrain cues remain source-visible and unexpanded.

### 008 — `வருவாய் வருவாய்...`

The village performance remains noisy, comic and deliberately coarse.

- source-anomalous forms `வைபோக`, `சைபோக`, `சைரந்திரியே`, and `மம்முதா` are transliterated rather than silently normalized;
- `கிச்சிலிப் பழம்` remains **kichili fruit**;
- `காச நோய்` remains the concrete **consumption** comparison;
- `டேய்` remains **Dei** to retain the taunting spoken force;
- monkey, moustache and tail-wagging ridicule remains direct;
- Bhimasena and the eight-directions/drum image remain intact.

### 009 — `புதியதோர் பாதை வகுப்போம்`

This is the batch's central rationalist/labour song. The English retains the complete conceptual chain:

- ignorance as barren waste;
- knowledge as a plough-point;
- science as the knowledge with which the field is ploughed;
- true wisdom as the sought result;
- change with changing times;
- springing knowledge and new tools;
- seedlings, weeds and grain-heads as actual field-work images;
- Nandanar's dream-miracle account contrasted directly with crops raised by **our hands and legs**;
- the ripened crop as **golden queen** and **comrade**;
- `பொதுக் களஞ்சியம்` as **common granary**.

The source's repetitions and colloquial agricultural forms remain structurally visible. Song `009` retains PDF 38–39 as one cross-page provenance record.

### 010 — `வாழ்க வாழ்க வாழ்க வாழ்கவே`

The translation preserves praise and political ridicule in the same song.

- the opening fourfold `வாழ்க` remains fourfold **Long live**;
- `அறிஞர்` is retained as **Arignar**;
- the buffalo-head-nodding image remains deliberately coarse;
- poor-as-worm / grandee-as-eagle remains a class contrast;
- widow-as-mouse / youth-as-sacrifice / old-custom-as-tiger remains an uncompressed reform sequence;
- `பகுத்தறிவு` remains **rational thought**;
- `பணத்தோட்டம்` and `செல்வக் குமரி... கோட்டம்!` are conservatively transliterated because the source may carry title/wordplay force that a literalized English phrase would over-interpret.

### 011 — `எதையும் தாங்கும் இதயம் வேண்டும்`

The political-philosophical exhortation remains stark:

- enmity/blame still strike like snakes;
- prison and torture remain explicit;
- `சாகாத காந்தி` remains **deathless Gandhi**;
- repeated `வாடாதே` remains repeated exhortation;
- life called honey still turns into a scorpion;
- `போர்முனைச் சாவே பொதுவாழ்வில் இன்பம்` remains **In public life, death at the battlefront is joy** rather than being softened into generic sacrifice/service language;
- arrogance, army, fury and slander remain explicit.
