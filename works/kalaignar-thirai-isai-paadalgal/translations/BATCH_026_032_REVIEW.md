## Song-by-song voice dispositions

### 026 — `வான் மலர் சோலையில்`

The romantic image-chain remains concrete rather than becoming generalized love prose.

- `வான் மலர் சோலை` remains a **sky-flower grove**;
- `தேன் மதி` remains a **honeyed moon**;
- the open subject in `மாருதில் ஓடுதே` remains open instead of being supplied by inference;
- jasmine creeper, companionship, overflowing dam, wave-surge, quenched fire, breeze, uncloying nectar and sweet mango-fruit remain separate images;
- `அணைமீறும் அன்பால் அலைமோதுகின்றாள்` retains both dam-overflow and wave-motion.

### 027 — `ஓடையிலே ஒருநாள்`

The bodily/musical dream sequence remains deliberately unusual.

- `இதழ் பறித்து` remains **plucked my lips** rather than being normalized into a conventional kiss line;
- north-wind chill and the intimate `டி தோழி` remain audible;
- the verified pressure point `எடுப்புக்க அமர்ந்தேன்` is conservatively retained as **sat down for the eduppu** rather than silently corrected;
- `பண்` and `யாழ்` remain **pann** and **yaazh**;
- the lover's sudden disappearance remains **like lightning**;
- no singer identity is added.

### 028 — `தமிழே தேனே கண்ணே தாலேலோ`

The lullaby keeps Tamil itself as an addressed presence.

- `தமிழே` remains **O Tamil**;
- `தாலேல / தாலல / தாலேலோ` remain source lullaby vocables rather than being replaced by English nursery sounds;
- golden lamp, wave-struck sea, mother who bore the child, moon, cloud-seeing peacock and answering dance remain intact;
- the closing `தூங்கப்பா` retains the colloquial `pa` force.

### 029 — `அலையிருக்குது கடலிலே`

The source's parallel `இருக்குது` architecture remains visible.

- wave/sea, movement/body and art/bend remain parallel;
- `விலையிருக்குதா` remains the repeated question **Is there a price in this world — for me?**;
- the printed split `பழக் / குலை` remains **fruit- / cluster** rather than being repaired or replaced by a generic sweetness metaphor;
- net-in-eyes and statue-before-me remain concrete;
- coquettish walk, gleaming waist, throbbing youth, arrow and pleasure-poem imagery remain distinct;
- `(அலை)` remains a refrain cue.

### 030 — `நீர்மேல் நடக்கலாம்`

The comic intoxication/sound-play register is retained rather than rationalized.

- walking on water, lying in fire and speaking whatever one thinks when one's state staggers remain explicit;
- world-spinning/body-spinning repetition remains intact;
- `கலகம் / கலயம்` is preserved as **kalagam / kalayam** sound-play with the meanings **commotion / pot** supplied alongside it rather than inventing an unrelated English pun;
- heaven/dizziness remains a direct intoxication joke;
- `இவ கன்னிப் பொண்ணு` remains colloquial **this maiden-girl**;
- `கன்னத்தில் ஒண்ணு` remains **one on the cheek** because the source does not explicitly name the implied act;
- exact source role labels remain traceable.

### 031 — `வெல்க நாடு வெல்க நாடு வெல்க வெல்கவே`

The martial exhortation remains forceful and source-shaped.

- repeated `வெல்க`, `செல்க`, `சென்று வா`, and `வென்று வா` remain repeated commands/exhortations;
- the warrior-conch blast, armies, giving one's head and guarding the mother's honour remain unsoftened;
- `தாய்` remains **mother** rather than being automatically expanded to motherland;
- hill-like shoulder, enemy feet, warriors' hands that do not pluck flowers, upraised sword, radiant eyes, `vengai` tiger-king and battlefield departure remain literal images;
- difficult verified source forms `குமலைப்`, `அஞ்சுகத்தின்`, and `புறப்படடா` are documented rather than normalized; `kumalai` and `Anjugam` remain conservative transliterations where the source does not support a unique correction.

### 032 — `ஒருவனுக்கு ஒருத்தி என்ற`

The moral-didactic structure is preserved without importing a different Thirukkural witness.

- `ஒருவனுக்கு ஒருத்தி` remains **one woman for one man**, not an abstract modern paraphrase;
- the anthology's printed `அறன் எனப் பட்டதே...` Thirukkural wording is translated as printed and is not silently harmonized with another edition;
- life remains a **boat**, and its lesson an unforgettable **Veda** in the human heart;
- youth remains a **costume that fades**;
- the repeated warning to guard before the storm remains repeated;
- the final oarless boat still goes wherever the waves call, and a life without someone to block evil is compared directly to it.
