## Purpose

This pilot establishes the English voice before scaling to all 54 songs. The user's governing instruction is to **retain Kalaignar's language in translation**.

The approved mode is `semantic-poetic-source-faithful`, not singable adaptation.

The pilot deliberately uses three different registers:

- `001` — rustic political/social satire;
- `002` — romantic duet, image chains and source turn labels;
- `003` — maternal lament, poverty/class contrast and rhetorical repetition.

## Voice decisions — song 001

`ஊருக்கு உழைப்பவண்டி` must remain a spoken, rustic social attack rather than become polished political prose.

Approved decisions:

- repeated colloquial `டி` force is selectively retained as `di`;
- `எருமைக் கன்னுக்குட்டி` remains the repeated **buffalo calf** image;
- the cheating-man / seven-storey-mansion / begging-man contrast stays explicit;
- leadership and wealth-taking language remains direct;
- `எகத்தாளம் போடுறானே` is rendered with performative swagger rather than neutralized;
- verified unusual forms `வேணசெல்வம்` and `பெண்ணி` are not used as excuses to rewrite Tamil. Their English renderings are conservative and the pressure points are recorded in notes.

The translation must preserve the song's mockery and class edge.

## Voice decisions — song 002

`இல் வாழ்வினிலே ஒளி ஏற்றும் தீபம்` tests whether English can retain Kalaignar's image density without converting it to generic romance.

Approved decisions:

- exact source turn labels `தங்`, `கல்`, `தங்,கல்` remain unexpanded;
- lamp/flame repetition remains repetition;
- `இளமயில்` remains **young peacock**;
- `கன்னல் தமிழே` remains **sugarcane-Tamil** rather than `sweet words`;
- `பனி தூங்கும் மலரே` remains **flower where dew sleeps**;
- `காந்தச் சிலையே` remains **magnetic statue** rather than being softened to `magnetic beauty`;
- `தேன் போல் வாழ்வோம்` remains **let us live like honey**.

These slightly unusual English images are intentional. Smoothing them away would erase the source's language.

## Voice decisions — song 003

`பூமாலை நீயே...` tests lament, source anomaly handling and social contrast.

Approved decisions:

- `சீரோடு வாழ்ந்தோம் / வேரோடு சாய்ந்தோம்` remains a tight splendour/roots reversal;
- `ராஜாங்கம்` / `ராஜா` imagery remains audible;
- `பாழான எந்தன் வயிற்றில்` is not softened;
- repeated `நீயேன் பிறந்தாய்?` remains repeated **Why were you born?**;
- bird-home / food-flying imagery remains concrete;
- `கோடானுகோடி` remains **crores upon crores** rather than generic `millions`;
- `சீமான்கள்` is rendered as **grandees** to preserve the poor-versus-privileged contrast.

The verified anthology reading `வந்தேன் தவழ்ந்தாய்?` is morphologically difficult. The pilot does **not** silently emend it to a familiar alternative; the English keeps the visible subject shift as `I came — did you crawl?` and records why.

## Translation gate for future songs

Future batches must follow these pilot rules:

1. retain source images before stylistic smoothing;
2. retain repeated lines and refrain architecture;
3. keep political, class and social critique direct;
4. preserve colloquial/cultural force where English has no exact equivalent;
5. never invent a source correction through translation;
6. never optimize for melody/rhyme at the cost of meaning;
7. keep attribution status separate from translation status.
