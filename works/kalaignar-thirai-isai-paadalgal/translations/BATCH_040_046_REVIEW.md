## Song-by-song voice dispositions

### 040 — `ஏய் கனவிலே மயங்காதே`

- `குறமுல்லை` remains **Kuramullai** rather than being replaced by a familiar alternate lyric;
- the refrain keeps **one day, embrace me / come, Raja** rather than smoothing the invitation;
- eyes as little flowers and hands seeking a woman's body remain direct;
- `சொல்லுக்கு செய்தாயே` is kept deliberately close as **you have done it to the word**, because the source does not safely supply a missing object;
- verified `பாட்டுப்பட்ட` is represented conservatively as **song-struck**;
- `வள்ளல்` remains **vallal**;
- the highly uncertain `ரம்மை போல கண்ணு ஜின்னை போல கண்ணும்` and `நாடி வரும் நாட்டி` are not repaired through soundtrack familiarity: **rammai / jinnai / naatti** remain documented pressure points.

### 041 — `ஒரு போராளியின் பயணமிது`

- the repeated worker/rest questions remain repeated and colloquial rather than becoming formal elegy;
- crawling in the mother's lap / sleeping beneath the ground remains a direct life-death reversal;
- `குட்டிச்சிங்கம்` remains **little lion** and `சுட்டித்தங்கம்` remains an intimate golden praise image;
- **murder-mad weapons of caste division** and the life taken for principle remain explicit political accusation;
- Jesus, Buddha, the Prophets, Gandhi, Periyar, Anna and Ambedkar remain in the anthology's printed order;
- `பேசும் புயல்` remains **speaking storm**, `குலவிளக்கு` remains **lamp of the lineage**, and the rough `பாரடா` ending retains **da**.

### 042 — `சுருளு மீசைக்காரனடி வேலுத்தம்பி`

- the folk vocative `டி` remains **di**;
- sun-fire, conspiracy-burning, clan-deity, golden-clan lion, earthquake-stage and powder-powder destruction remain source-shaped;
- verified `ஏறிமலையில்` is not silently corrected: **eeri-malai** remains a documented pressure point;
- the `குழு` section retains repeated **kummi** and **kulavai** performance vocabulary;
- `தம்பி` remains **thambi**;
- `தனியுடைமை / பொதுவுடைமை` remains the explicit ideological contrast **private ownership / common ownership**;
- `தும்பி` remains **thumbi** rather than forcing a single English species label.

### 043 — `ஆற, அமர கொஞ்சம் யோசிச்சுப் பாரு`

- the opening campaign quotation is preserved separately and no outside campaign text is imported;
- `ஆற, அமர` remains a paired **calmly, at ease** instruction rather than a generic `think carefully` paraphrase;
- the rise/fall/weights image remains a weighing metaphor;
- worker versus town-cheater, vote-buying wretches and **bribe-demons** remain direct;
- the source's compact `தியாகம் செஞ்ச வரை மோசம் செஞ்சவரு` is not silently rewritten into corrected Tamil;
- rice-price and vote-price rhetoric remains literal;
- `உறவுக்கு கரம் / உரிமைக்கு குரல்` remains **hand for kinship / voice for rights**;
- `தம்பி`, `தென்பாண்டி சிங்கம்`, and Kalaignar's politically resonant `உடன் பிறப்பே` remain **thambi / Thenpandi lion / udanpirappe**.

### 044 — `காவலுக்கு கெட்டிக்காரன்`

- `காக்கிச்சட்டைக்காரன்` remains the colloquial **khaki-shirt man**, not a bureaucratic `police officer`;
- the do-what-we-said / say-only-what-we-did pairing remains explicit;
- `மாமூலு` remains **mamool** as the culture-specific bribe/regular-payment term;
- the fence grazing the crop remains literal;
- the goat / `vengai` image remains source-shaped;
- illicit-liquor `support` code-mixing, whip imagery, and `பஞ்சு பஞ்சா பிச்சிடுவான்` remain colloquial and forceful rather than being softened into administrative anti-corruption prose.

### 045 — `தங்கக்குணம் படைத்த எங்கள் தந்தையே`

- the filial elegy keeps tears bathing the father's feet;
- `கைவிரல்களையே விழிகளாக்கி` remains the striking **made our very fingers into eyes**, without importing explanatory context absent from the lyric;
- `கருணைக்கடலே / பொறுமையின் இமயமே` remains **ocean of compassion / Himalaya of patience**, repeated as printed;
- the mixed metaphor of hearts as flowers, a garland, and the **neck of the summit of your fame** is not regularized;
- the final collective plea for forgiveness remains direct.

### 046 — `நீதிமன்றத் துலாக்கோலே சாயலாமா`

- the court's tilted balance-scale and repeated innocent-death question remain direct;
- `பாவமோரிடம் பழியோரிடம்` remains **sin with one / blame with another**;
- `அமாவாசை` remains **amavasai** rather than a flattened darkness metaphor;
- predator/goat and fence/grazing-crop images remain literal;
- `அன்னைத் தமிழகமே` remains **Mother Tamilagam**;
- the closing verified anomaly `வாழுக்குவேலை / வாழுக்கு வேலை` is deliberately retained as **vaazhukku-velai / vaazhukku velai** and is **not** silently emended to a familiar alternate phrase such as `வாளுக்கு வேலை`.
