## Special structural/editorial cases

### மந்திரிகுமாரி — unnumbered censored/prohibited-song mention

PDF 25 states that Kalaignar wrote two songs for the film and names:

1. `ஆளப்பிறந்தவன் தமிழன் அவன்தானே`
2. `ஊருக்கு உழைப்பவண்டி (எரும கன்னுக்குட்டி)`

The prose states that the first song was censored/prohibited. The anthology does not print it as one of the numbered lyric records. Therefore it remains an **editorial/prose-mentioned song**, outside numbered `001–054`.

### பராசக்தி — item-level anthology attribution

PDF 28 lists the film's songs and places `கலைஞர்` beside:

- `இல் வாழ்வினிலே ஒளி ஏற்றும் தீபம்`;
- `பூமாலை நீயே`.

This is item-level attribution **within this anthology**. It does not overwrite or strengthen the authority of the already archived `works/parasakthi/` source layer without a separate source-to-source comparison.
